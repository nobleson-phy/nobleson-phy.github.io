abstract: >
  We present a novel method for high-precision pulsar timing based on reconstructing pulse profiles from their temporally stable emission components. This technique effectively handles profile variability while maintaining the precision required for gravitational wave detection in pulsar timing arrays. We demonstrate the method on PSR J1713+0747, one of the most precisely timed millisecond pulsars in the IPTA, showing improved timing residuals and noise characterization.
